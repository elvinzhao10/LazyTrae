## Historical records

The remaining files in this directory preserve earlier plans, prompts, designs, evaluations, migration notes, reports, and archived dogfood material. They are retained for provenance and study only; they are not current operating instructions and must not be treated as a dependency of a v0.15 installation.

- `archive/`, `plan/`, and `prompts/` contain dated records.
- `design/` and `reference/` capture prior analysis and design material.
- The repository `NOTICE` and `LICENSE` are the authoritative legal and attribution records.
